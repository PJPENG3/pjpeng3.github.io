--- layout: single title: "Teaching" permalink: /teaching/ author_profile: true --- {% capture teaching %} I have been a teaching assistant in the School of Industrial and Systems Engineering at the University of Oklahoma since 2024. ## ISE 4804 – Ergonomics in Systems Design *Lab instructor · Spring 2025, Spring 2026* I teach the weekly lab sessions for this course. Students work in teams of three or four and write a technical report or executive summary for each lab. **Lab design.** I worked with the course instructor to plan and update the labs. The lab topics include: - How to write a good technical report - Evaluating the usability of a website (procedural and hierarchical task analysis) - Redesigning a pickup truck driver's workspace (structural anthropometry) - Risk assessment for manual lifting tasks (NIOSH lifting equation and Snook tables) - Evaluating and redesigning a drone control interface (KLM-GOMS and cognitive walkthrough) **Generative AI in the labs.** In Spring 2026 we added generative AI to the labs. The goal is to help students use AI well, not to let it do the work for them. I helped draft the plan: - Each lab focuses on one type of AI use. Examples are literature search, task planning, proofreading, and building a quick interface mock-up. - Students submit the prompts they used. They also write a short review of the AI output: what helped, what was wrong, and what they changed. ## ISE 4663/5663 – Systems Analysis Using Simulation *Teaching assistant · Fall 2025, Fall 2026* - Teach six computer lab sessions.
